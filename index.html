  • Number in pin = road minutes to downtown Knoxville.
  • Pin size = acreage.
  • Shopping = real road time to the nearest Walmart, Kroger, Food City, Ingles, Publix, ALDI or Target — a real town, not a country store.
  • Terrain is the average slope of the hillside around the pin. Anything over 20° is dropped before it reaches this map.
  • Solid pin sits on the real parcel location.
  • Dashed pin is placed at the town centre — exact parcel unknown.
  • Rings are straight-line 15/30/45 min approximations; the pin numbers are the real thing.
